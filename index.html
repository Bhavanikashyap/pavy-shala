Welcome to Ashtanga Yoga with Sheshadri and Harish

An Institute to teach Ashtanga Vinyasa Yoga in Mysuru.

Inspired by Sri T. Krishnamacharya, Sri B.K.S. Iyengar, Sri B.N.S. Iyengar and Sri K. Pattabhi Jois, PAVY Shala offers traditional Ashtanga Vinyasa Yoga and Pranayama through the experience, dedication and guidance of Sheshadri and Harish.

Sheshadri — Founder

One of the most experienced, world-renowned and certified teachers in Ashtanga Vinyasa Yoga.

Harish — Director

An experienced, world-renowned and certified teacher in Ashtanga Vinyasa Yoga.

Sheshadri and his son Harish give maximum personal attention to every practitioner, providing useful advice and helpful adjustments and allowing students a deeper experience of the various Asanas and Pranayama, helping them develop and deepen their practice in a sensitive manner according to their individual capacity.

OUR AIM

Traditional Practice for Every Practitioner

Our aim is to preserve and share the traditional practice of Mysuru Ashtanga Vinyasa Yogasana and Pranayama, offering personal guidance and a supportive learning environment for both beginners and advanced yoga practitioners.
