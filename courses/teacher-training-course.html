Teacher Training Course (TTC)

An intensive and in-depth Ashtanga Vinyasa Yoga teacher training course designed to deepen personal practice, understand sequencing and develop the skills required to guide practitioners at varied levels.

ABOUT THE COURSE

Deepen Your Practice. Develop Your Teaching.

The Ashtanga Vinyasa Yoga Teacher Training Course is an intensive and in-depth learning program designed to deepen the practitioner’s personal practice and develop a clear understanding of the principles of teaching. Students explore the importance of asana sequencing and learn to approach the practice with clarity, awareness, discipline and sensitivity.

The course gives particular attention to breathing during asana practice and pranayama, helping students understand how breath, movement and awareness work together to support a steady and positive practice. Through guided practice, observation and study, practitioners develop yogic skills and the confidence to work appropriately with students of different levels of experience and ability.

The training is structured progressively so that students can strengthen their own practice while learning how to observe, guide and support practitioners in a responsible and thoughtful manner.
